# Live Demo & Flow
## Objective
- Primary goal is to observe your ownership of the project.
- This session evaluates how you navigate your own application and whether you prioritize user experience and system reliability over just "making it work"
  
## What we're looking for
- Logical presentation: Your ability to lead a structured, coherent walkthrough of the application.
- Edge Case Awareness: Your proactive demonstration of boundary conditions and error states, not just the "happy path"
- Self-Correction: Your ability to identify and acknowledge current limitations or areas for future improvement.
