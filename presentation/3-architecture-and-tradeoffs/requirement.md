# Architecture & Trade-offs
## Objective:
- The goal of this discussion is to evaluate your technical depth and critical thinking. We want to see if you understand the long-term consequences of your architectural decisions, specifically regarding scale and concurrency.
  
## What we're looking for
- System stress-testing: Your ability to reason through how the system behaves under load (e.g 10k+ tasks) or high concurrency
- Second-order thinking: Your ability to trace the "ripple effects" of a design change across the entire stack.
- Risk-based testing: Your strategic approach to testing, focusing on high-risk logic (like recurrence calculations) rather than trivial CRUD operations.
