# Requirement Interpretation
## Objective
- This module assesses your ability to navigate ambiguity - a key indicator of seniority.
- We aim to understand your mental model for clarifying underspecified business needs before moving to execution.

## What we're looking for
- Ambiguity identification: Your ability to spot non-obvious conflicts in requirements (e.g how "soft delete" affectactive dependencies)
- Downstream reasoning: Your understanding of how a specific interpretation of a requirement impacts features furthedown the road.
- Intentionality: Your justification for why you choose one interpretation over alternatives.
